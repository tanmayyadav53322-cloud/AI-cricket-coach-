Cricket Technique Fundamentals

This page is a short hub for the core technique concepts referenced across AI Cricket Coach — each one is explained fully on the relevant analysis page or the glossary.

  • Batting stance — How a batter sets up before the ball is bowled — foot position, balance and bat position.
  • Footwork — The movement of the feet in response to the ball — front-foot and back-foot movement for batters, run-up and delivery-stride positioning for bowlers.
  • Head position — How still and level the head stays through a shot or delivery.
  • Weight transfer — The shift of a batter's weight between the back and front foot during a shot.
  • Bat path — The swing of the bat through a shot, including the line it travels and the follow-through.
  • Follow-through — The finishing motion of a shot or delivery after the ball leaves the bat or hand.
  • Run-up — A bowler's approach to the crease before delivering the ball.
  • Delivery stride — The final stride into the crease just before a bowler releases the ball.
  • Bowling action — The overall sequence of movement a bowler uses to deliver the ball, from run-up to follow-through.

For a full walkthrough, see batting technique analysis, bowling action analysis, the cricket coaching glossary, or training drills for each area.
