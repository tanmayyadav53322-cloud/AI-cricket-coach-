Cricket Coaching Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the cricket technique and video-analysis terms used across AI Cricket Coach.

Batting stance
How a batter sets up before the ball is bowled — foot position, balance and bat position. Learn more
Footwork
The movement of the feet in response to the ball — front-foot and back-foot movement for batters, run-up and delivery-stride positioning for bowlers. Learn more
Head position
How still and level the head stays through a shot or delivery. Learn more
Weight transfer
The shift of a batter's weight between the back and front foot during a shot. Learn more
Bat path
The swing of the bat through a shot, including the line it travels and the follow-through. Learn more
Follow-through
The finishing motion of a shot or delivery after the ball leaves the bat or hand. Learn more
Run-up
A bowler's approach to the crease before delivering the ball. Learn more
Delivery stride
The final stride into the crease just before a bowler releases the ball. Learn more
Bowling action
The overall sequence of movement a bowler uses to deliver the ball, from run-up to follow-through. Learn more
Pose estimation
A computer-vision technique that locates key points on a person's body — such as shoulders, hips and knees — in each frame of a video. Learn more
Movement analysis
The process of studying body movement over a sequence of video frames, used here to describe cricket-specific actions. Learn more
Confidence level
On AI Cricket Coach, a High/Medium/Low label attached to each observation, reflecting how clearly the video supports it. Learn more
Gather
The short transition phase where a bowler gathers balance between the run-up and the delivery stride. Learn more
Release point
The position of a bowler's hand at the moment the ball is released. Learn more
