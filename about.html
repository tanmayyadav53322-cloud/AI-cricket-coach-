About AI Cricket Coach

AI Cricket Coach is ai-powered cricket video analysis and training platform. Players upload or record a batting or bowling video; the app is designed to turn it into structured, cricket-specific feedback and a training plan, using computer vision to observe visible technique rather than guesswork.

Where things stand today

Player profiles, video recording, upload, and validation (length, size, resolution, lighting) are live. The AI video-analysis engine — the computer-vision step that produces batting and bowling feedback — is under active development. Until it's connected, the app says so plainly rather than showing invented results; see the Limitations section on the homepage for the current state.

Our approach

AI Cricket Coach is built as a complement to human coaching, not a replacement for it: it doesn't diagnose injuries, doesn't guarantee performance improvement, and is explicit about what a video simply can't show. See how AI cricket video analysis works for the detail behind that approach.
