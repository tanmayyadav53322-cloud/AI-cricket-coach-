Cricket Training Drills

These are general cricket coaching drills anyone can use, organized by discipline. They're educational content — not personalized to your own video — and mirror the training library built into the AI Cricket Coach app. For drills matched to your own analysis, open the app and create a 7-day training plan.

Batting drills

  • Front-foot drive — Step to the pitch of the ball, head over the front knee, and drive along the line.
  • Back-foot movement — Move back and across, stay balanced, and play the ball late.
  • Defensive technique — Soft hands, bat close to the pad, head still.
  • Cover drive — Front foot to the pitch, high elbow, full follow-through.
  • Head-position drill — Play drop-fed balls and hold your finish with the head over the front knee.

See related technique analysis.

Fast Bowling drills

  • Run-up rhythm — Repeat your run-up at 70% pace and hit the same marks each time.
  • Front-leg stability — Land with a braced front leg and hold the finish for 2 seconds.
  • Alignment drill — Keep hips and shoulders side-on at landing, using a line as a guide.
  • Release drill — Bowl from a standing start at half pace and release at the highest point.

See related technique analysis.

Spin Bowling drills

  • Wrist and finger control — Practise your grip and spin with a slow, controlled release.
  • Accuracy drill — Place a marker on a good length and aim to land most balls on it.
  • Flight and variation practice — Change pace and height of release while keeping the same action.

See related technique analysis.

Wicketkeeping drills

  • Stance and crouch hold — Stay balanced on the balls of your feet with head still and hands together.
  • Glove-work catches — Catch soft tosses with soft hands and give with the ball.
  • Footwork to the ball — Move sideways to the line, stay low, and take the ball late.

See related technique notes.

Fielding drills

  • Ready-position start — Stay low on the balls of your feet and take a quick first step.
  • Pickup and throw — Field a rolling ball, step, and throw to a target.
  • Throwing mechanics — Point your front shoulder at the target and finish with your chest facing it.

See related technique notes.

Fitness drills

  • Mobility — Hip circles, trunk rotations and shoulder mobility at easy effort.
  • Core — Planks and side planks with a neutral spine.
  • Lower body — Squats and lunges with a controlled tempo.

See related technique notes.

Warm up before drills, use a controlled tempo, and stop if anything hurts. See a qualified coach or medical professional for persistent pain.
