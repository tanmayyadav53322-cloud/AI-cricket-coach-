Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Cricket Coach?

AI Cricket Coach is an AI-powered cricket video analysis and training platform for cricket players. Players upload or record a batting or bowling video and get structured technique observations, drills, and a training plan.

Who is AI Cricket Coach for?

Cricket players of any level — beginner, intermediate or advanced — across batting, fast bowling, spin bowling, wicketkeeping and fielding, who want a structured way to review their own technique between coaching sessions.

What is AI cricket video analysis?

The use of computer vision, specifically pose estimation, to observe a player's body position and movement in a video and describe it in cricket-coaching terms. Read more on the AI cricket video analysis page.

How does cricket video analysis work?

You upload or record a video, the app validates its length, size and quality, a pose-estimation model observes visible movement, and the results are organized into strengths, possible issues with confidence levels, and drills. See how it works.

How can I improve my batting technique?

Practice fundamentals like stance, head position and footwork through shadow batting and throwdowns, and review your own footage regularly. See batting analysis and drills.

What does batting video analysis measure?

Visible elements only: stance and balance, head position, footwork, weight transfer, bat path, contact position when visible, and follow-through.

How can I improve cricket footwork?

For batters, moving early and decisively into position for a shot; for bowlers, a consistent run-up rhythm and stable front-leg landing. Slow, repetition-based practice comes before adding pace or match intensity.

What is a good batting stance?

A commonly taught baseline is a balanced, side-on stance with even weight distribution, soft knees, a still head, and the bat resting near the back toe — adapted to suit the individual player.

How can video analysis improve cricket training?

It gives a consistent, repeatable record of technique over time, instead of relying on memory or a single in-person session, which makes it easier to track whether a specific change is actually happening.

Does AI Cricket Coach replace a real cricket coach?

No. It's designed to support practice between sessions and give players a structured way to review their own video — not to replace a qualified coach's judgment or hands-on feedback.

Does AI Cricket Coach diagnose injuries?

No. It does not diagnose injuries, assess injury risk, or give medical advice. For pain or a suspected injury, see a qualified coach or a medical professional.

Is my uploaded video private?

Yes. Videos are uploaded to private, per-account storage with access rules that only allow the account owner to read, insert or delete their own files and records.

What video length and format do I need?

Between 3 and 90 seconds, under 200 MB, ideally 480p or higher, in a common format such as MP4, MOV or WebM, with your full body visible where possible.

Is AI Cricket Coach free to use?

Pricing and paid plans have not been finalized. The focus right now is making the core upload-to-feedback flow work well before introducing any paid tier.
