What is AI Cricket Coach?

AI Cricket Coach is an AI-powered cricket video analysis and training platform for cricket players. You record or upload a batting or bowling video, and the platform is built to turn it into cricket-specific technique observations, drills and a personalized training plan. See how it works.

Who is it for?

Beginner, intermediate and advanced players — batters, fast and spin bowlers, wicketkeepers and fielders — who want a structured way to review their own technique between coaching sessions.

How it works

  1. Upload

    Upload your batting or bowling video.

  2. Analyze

    AI analyzes visible body movement and cricket technique.

  3. Understand

    Receive strengths, possible issues, and explanations.

  4. Train

    Follow personalized drills and a weekly training plan.

What can be analyzed?

Five analysis types: Batting, Fast Bowling, Spin Bowling, Wicketkeeping and Fielding. Analysis looks at movement a camera can actually show, using pose estimation, and reports how confident each observation is. Learn about AI cricket video analysis.

Batting analysis

Stance and balance, head position, footwork, weight transfer, bat path and follow-through. Read about batting technique analysis.

Bowling analysis

Run-up rhythm, delivery stride, front-leg position, arm action, release position when visible, and follow-through for fast and spin bowling. Read about bowling action analysis.

Personalized training

Drills and a 7-day plan matched to your role, level, goal and analysis results. Anyone can also browse general cricket training drills.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI Cricket Coach?

AI Cricket Coach is an AI-powered cricket video analysis and training platform for cricket players. Players record or upload a batting or bowling video to get technique observations, drills and a training plan.

How does cricket video analysis work?

You upload or record a video, the app checks its length, size and quality, and a pose-estimation model observes visible movement such as stance, footwork or arm action. Results are grouped into strengths, possible issues with confidence levels, and drills.

Does AI Cricket Coach replace a real cricket coach?

No. It is designed to support practice between coaching sessions and does not replace a qualified coach or a medical professional.

Does it diagnose injuries?

No. It does not diagnose injuries or give medical advice. For pain or a suspected injury, see a qualified coach or a medical professional.

Is AI video analysis available yet?

Not yet. Video recording, upload and quality checks are live. The AI analysis engine is under development, and the app shows that analysis is unavailable instead of inventing feedback.

More questions and answers

Limitations

  • AI video analysis is not available yet; the app says so instead of showing made-up feedback.
  • Analysis uses a single 2D video, so it cannot measure ball speed, spin revolutions or internal joint loading.
  • Observations carry High, Medium or Low confidence, and unclear movements are reported as unable to assess reliably.
  • It does not diagnose injuries, guarantee improvement, or replace a qualified cricket coach or medical professional.
